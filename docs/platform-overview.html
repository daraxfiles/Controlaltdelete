01 — Overview

What is CTRL+ALT+MEDIA?

CTRL+ALT+MEDIA is a digital platform built for young people who want to investigate information failures in their communities, verify what's true, and publish accountability journalism that actually reaches decision-makers.

The name says it all: CTRL — take control of the narrative. ALT — build the alternative. MEDIA — do it in public.

The platform is organized around a structured 7-stage investigative process called The Reboot Protocol. Every crew moves through those stages together — from identifying a broken information system, all the way to publishing a verified Media Patch and sending a Power Ping to the people responsible for fixing it.

03 — Core Workflow

The 7-Stage Reboot Protocol

Every investigation on the platform moves through seven stages. Each stage has clear deliverables. You cannot publish a Media Patch until you have completed an Evidence Receipt.

01
Crash Report
Identify the Information Failure
Name the specific way the information system has broken down. What are people not being told? Who is being left out of the story?
02
System Trace
Map Who Controls the Story
Trace the flow of information. Which institutions produce it? Which outlets distribute it? Who profits from the current narrative?
03
Red Team Room
Challenge Your Own Story
Before you publish, stress-test your evidence. What could you be wrong about? What counterarguments exist? Document your uncertainties honestly.
04
Build the Patch
Create the Alternative Media
Produce your verified counter-narrative — article, photo essay, podcast, data story, community resource guide, or digital zine.
05
Ship With Receipts
File Your Evidence Receipt
Before publishing, document every claim, source, interview, and verification step in the Evidence Receipt Builder. No receipt, no reboot.
06
Power Ping
Send Evidence to Decision-Makers
Draft three specific questions for the person with power to fix the problem. Send the Power Ping and track whether they respond.
07
Patch Notes
Publish Your Media Patch
Your verified patch enters the public archive. Community readers can Verify, Amplify, and Apply it. The institutional response is tracked here.

05 — Getting Started

How to use the platform

For youth journalists starting their first investigation:

Create an account

Click Start Reboot in the navigation bar. Sign up with email or Google. The first person to join earns the Founding Crew badge permanently.

Set up your crew profile

Go to your Dashboard and add your display name, crew name, and bio. This appears publicly alongside your published patches.

Pick a mission to build your skills

Visit Missions and start with a Beginner mission like Headline Autopsy or Search Yourself. Complete the deliverables with your crew and mark it complete in your Dashboard.

Launch a Reboot investigation

Click + New Reboot and complete the 4-step intake form. This creates your project and places it at Stage 01 (Crash Report) of the Reboot Protocol.

Work through the 7 stages

Follow the Reboot Protocol on the Reboot Protocol page. Use the Evidence Receipt Builder before you publish. File a Power Ping at Stage 06.

Publish your Media Patch

Once your facilitator approves the patch in the Admin Panel, it enters the public archive at Media Patches. Community readers can Verify, Amplify, and Apply it in the Reboot Room.

For community readers (no account needed):

Browse the Media Patches archive

Go to /media-patches to see all published investigations. Filter by topic or media type.

Respond in the Reboot Room

Visit /reboot-room to Verify a patch (confirm its accuracy from your experience), Amplify it (add context and share), or Apply it (commit to using the information in your own work or community).

Check the Evidence Receipt

Every published patch links to its public Evidence Receipt — a complete record of every claim and source. If something seems off, note it in your Reboot Room response.

07 — Platform Principles

How we do journalism here

No receipt, no reboot

Every published patch requires a completed Evidence Receipt. Transparency about sources, verification steps, and uncertainties is not optional — it is the product.

Zero starts honestly

The platform launches empty. No fake stats or sample patches. Patch #001 is genuinely unassigned. The archive starts the day the first crew publishes their first investigation.

Power must respond

A Media Patch without a Power Ping is incomplete. Every published investigation includes three specific questions sent directly to the person with the power to fix the problem.

Safety is non-negotiable

Each mission and investigation includes safety notes. Youth journalists never identify private individuals without consent and never put personal information into AI tools.
